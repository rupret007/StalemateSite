Crapjack 21

Beat the dealer without busting. Manage bankroll, pick smart actions, and use house rules to your advantage.

On phones, tap the on-screen pit buttons:

  • Deal: start a hand
  • Hit: take another card
  • Stand: keep your hand
  • Double: double your bet (first action only)
  • Split: play two hands (when pair eligible)
  • Surrender: give up half your bet (if enabled)
  • Clear Bet (Clear on phones): remove your bet before dealing

Keyboard (optional): N deal · H hit · S stand · D double · P split · X surrender · C clear bet.

Tip: find Table Setup and strategy hints in Table Intel (Table Menu → Intel → Strategy, history & rules on phones).
