    How TurdRummy Works

    Goal: first to 100 by making melds and minimizing deadwood.

    • Set = 3-4 of the same rank.
    • Run = 3+ consecutive cards in one suit.
    • Knock when your post-discard deadwood is 10 or less.
    • Gin when post-discard deadwood is exactly 0.
    • Undercut gives bonus points to the defender.

    Your hand groups melds under coloured brackets; grey cards are deadwood. The Knock button glows when a knock is legal, and Gin shimmers when gin is possible.

    On mobile, draw/discard/knock/gin are grouped in one button dock. Bot difficulty (Easy, Normal, Sharp) is in the top bar.
