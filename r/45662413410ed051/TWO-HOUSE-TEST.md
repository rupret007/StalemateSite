# Hang Out: two-house test checklist (for Jeff)
Hidden preview. Don't share the link outside the test group.

**Setup**
- House A: Jeff, home Wi-Fi, laptop (Chrome or Safari) and an iPhone.
- House B: a friend on a different internet provider, a laptop or Android phone.
- Optional: one phone on cellular with Wi-Fi off. That's the hardest case.

**Run it** (about 30 minutes)
1. **Start a room:** Jeff opens the preview link and taps **Start a room**. Then tap **Copy invite link** and text it to House B. ☐ It opens in under 3 seconds and needs no account.
2. **Join:** House B taps the link and taps **Jump in**. ☐ Both see "2 here" with names. ☐ No camera or mic prompt shows up yet.
3. **Chat:** say something each way. ☐ It shows up within a second.
4. **Draw:** both draw at the same time. ☐ Strokes show up on the other screen right away. ☐ Jeff's **Wipe it** clears both screens. ☐ House B doesn't see a Wipe button.
5. **Bob Ross:** Jeff taps **Bob Ross: paint along**. ☐ It plays on both. ☐ Pause, scrub ahead and play again: the other side follows within about a second. ☐ After 20 minutes the two are still within about a second of each other.
6. **Manic:** Jeff taps **Manic (Stalemate)**. ☐ It plays from the site on both.
7. **Any link:** Jeff pastes any YouTube link and taps **Play for everyone**. ☐ It works.
8. **Late joiner:** a third device joins midway. ☐ It sees the current drawing and video at the right spot, with no earlier chat. ☐ New messages arrive once. ☐ Disconnect/reconnect starts a fresh chat buffer while an unsent draft remains.
9. **Faces:** each person taps **Turn on my camera**, then **Turn on my mic**. ☐ A plain explainer comes first, then the browser asks. ☐ Saying "Not now" keeps it off. ☐ Faces show as small bubbles. ☐ Voices are clear with headphones. ☐ Note any echo without headphones.
10. **Phone:** do steps 2–9 on the iPhone too. ☐ It's usable one-handed. ☐ Video starts with one tap if iOS blocks autoplay ("Tap to sync in").
11. **Cellular:** repeat faces with the cellular phone. ☐ Did faces connect? If not, write down "no direct connection". That tells us whether we need the relay.
12. **Leaving:** close a tab. ☐ The others' count drops and that person's face bubble disappears.

**Write down:** devices and browsers, what felt clunky, anything confusing, battery drain after 30 minutes, and whether faces connected on every pair.
